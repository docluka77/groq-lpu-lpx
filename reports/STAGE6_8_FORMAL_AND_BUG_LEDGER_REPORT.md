## 3. Stage 8 Full-Chip Formal Verification (`formal/groq_lpu_top_formal_bind.sv`)
In Stage 8, we constructed `formal/groq_lpu_top_formal_bind.sv` (and `ifdef FORMAL_TOP` invariants in `rtl/groq_lpu_top.sv`) parameterized with `P_MEM_ADDR_W = 3` (`BANK_DEPTH = 8`) and proved four top-level full-chip architectural safety invariants across all 8 spatial slices (`SLICE_NIM`, `SLICE_MEM_W`, `SLICE_SXM`, `SLICE_VXM`, `SLICE_MXM_W`, `SLICE_ICU`, `SLICE_MXM_E`, `SLICE_MEM_E`):

1. **Global Stream Fabric Determinism & Zero-Stall Invariant**:
   - Proved `fab_east_consume_en == '0` and `fab_west_consume_en == '0` across all 8 slices, and proved that for every East hop $s \in [1..7]$ and West hop $s \in [0..6]$, `stream_valid_east_o[s] == ($past(fab_east_inject_en[s]) | $past(stream_valid_east_o[s-1]))` and `stream_valid_west_o[s] == ($past(fab_west_inject_en[s]) | $past(stream_valid_west_o[s+1]))`.
2. **Top-Level Barrier & Halt Safety**:
   - Proved that whenever `barrier_waiting_o[s] || slice_halted_o[s]` holds on any slice $s \in [0..7]$, `!slice_vliw_valid_o[s] && !imem_fetch_en_o[s]`, and specifically when `SLICE_MEM_W` or `SLICE_MEM_E` is waiting at a barrier or halted (`!host_mem_w_wr_en_i`, `!host_mem_e_wr_en_i`), `!mem_w_wr_valid` and `!mem_e_wr_valid` hold unconditionally.
3. **Top-Level SECDED Single-Bit Fault Containment**:
   - Proved that any single-bit error mask (`$onehot(mem_err_inject_mask)`) injected into `u_mem_w` or `u_mem_e` across all 144 codeword bits (`2 x 72b` Hsiao SECDED subwords) is corrected in 1 cycle and **never** asserts `ecc_double_err_o` (`ecc_double_err_o == 2'b00`).
4. **Top-Level Power Telemetry Bounds & Stream Decay**:
   - Proved `zero_skip_count_w_o <= 5'd16` and `zero_skip_count_e_o <= 5'd16` on every cycle, and proved that after $k$ consecutive cycles without fabric stream injection (`f_top_no_inj_cnt_q > k`), `stream_valid_east_o[k] == '0` and `stream_valid_west_o[7 - k] == '0` (complete 8-hop decay to zero within 8 cycles).
